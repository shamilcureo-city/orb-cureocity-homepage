# Orb conversion direction

The visitor is a busy adult who wants a workable food and training routine. The commercial question is whether Orb is worth ₹7,500 annually; the user confirmed that price in this conversation. Professional care remains optional and separately described, with no invented inclusions or outcomes.

## Design plan

Palette: original Midnight #0b1427, Ink #172335, Cloud #f6f8fb, Ice #e5eef8, Coral #df526d, White #ffffff. Manrope carries the compact conversational headings, Inter carries body and product text. Retain the original logo, handset, meal, workout and coral Orb artwork. No replacement stock imagery or generic tracking-score walls.

Layout: a benefit-led original hero, followed immediately by an interactive week that resolves a familiar practical obstacle. A compact product value spread uses the original meal/workout phones; a visible two-sided plan update shows the professional care route. One concrete annual offer, objections and a short final action complete the story. Record verification and permission details move to optional expandable content, preserving truth while reducing administrative weight.

[Promise + annual price] [useful daily phone]

[Food + movement product evidence]
[Optional professional continuity: feedback -> reviewed change]
[₹7,500/year offer + clearly separate care]
[FAQs + Get Orb]

## Review against brief

The prior version's generic connected-data headline and error-correction centrepiece made it read like documentation. This version leads with practical output, an explicit price, and one coherent story. The recurring value is a plan connected to what actually happened, not an invented guarantee or exclusive competitor claim. The hypothetical week is clearly labelled. No implied free tier, trial, discount, clinical result or bundled consultation. Final feature eligibility, live app evidence and store links still require confirmation. Preserve owner-private audience.
