What can I try for free?+

Start with the free diet-plan builder or free fitness assessment. These are separate from the Orb annual subscription and optional professional care.

Choose your free first step
I already use a wearable. Why would I add Orb?+

A wearable contributes one part of your story. Orb’s connected approach brings available device trends alongside your reports, goals, meals, workouts and personal check-ins. The value to look for is how that context supports your daily plan and next review. Check whether your device and the information you want to use are supported.

See how your information comes together
Do I need a wearable to begin?+

No. You can begin with your goals, records and your own daily inputs. Device-based trends and scores need the relevant supported data and permissions. Missing data should stay visibly missing, rather than being treated as a zero or a missed activity.

Can I use reports I already have?+

The Health Vault experience is designed around existing reports: keep the original file, review extracted details and correct them before they become verified information. Supported formats and limits need to be confirmed in the available app. New tests are not a requirement for everyone.

Explore Health Vault
Does Orb replace my doctor or make treatment decisions?+

No. Orb’s AI supports organisation, explanation and planning from available information. A pattern can suggest a useful question; it does not establish a diagnosis or its cause. Clinical assessment, diagnosis and treatment remain with appropriately qualified professionals.

What if I already have a dietitian’s or trainer’s plan?+

Existing professional advice is relevant context. Within an eligible Orb care service, the active professional plan and its restrictions guide the digital experience. Your logs and feedback can inform the next review; AI should not independently change clinical targets or treatment.

See a connected care example
Can my family see all of my health information?+

No. The Family Circle concept uses an accepted invitation and your choice of high-level summaries. It is not unrestricted access to your records or raw sensor data. You retain control over sharing and revocation. Family Circle release and availability remain subject to confirmation.

Explore the permission-based example
Do I need a Blueprint assessment before starting?+

No. You can begin with your existing information. Blueprint is an optional assessment route that brings together findings, professional discussions and recommended next steps. Check the selected package for tests, consultations, location and follow-up.

Understand the assessment route
Are professional consultations included in ₹7,500?+

Professional care is optional and separate from the app subscription. Confirm the professional, consultations, follow-up, current price and location availability before choosing a care service. Orb’s AI guidance does not replace clinical assessment or treatment.

See how professional care connects
What happens after I download Orb?+

Explore the available subscription, review its features and terms, then choose your plan in the app. Start with your goals and the information you have. Add supported connections or professional care when relevant to you.

Is ₹625 a monthly subscription?+

No. The annual price is ₹7,500, billed annually. ₹625 is its monthly equivalent, not a separate monthly payment option. Check the final billing, renewal and cancellation terms in the app before subscribing.
