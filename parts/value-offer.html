A few straightforward answers.

I already track my health. What would I use Orb for?+

Use Orb to put your information to work in your routine: planning food and movement, recording what happens and bringing that context into your next review. A supported wearable can add information to that experience. You do not have to replace the device you already use.

Do I need a wearable to begin?+

No. You can start with your goals, everyday habits and existing information. Device-based features need the relevant supported data and permissions; check the available integrations inside Orb.

Are professional consultations included in ₹7,500?+

Professional care is optional and separate from the app subscription. Confirm the professional, consultations, follow-up, current price and location availability before choosing a care service. Orb’s AI guidance does not replace clinical assessment or treatment.

What happens after I download Orb?+

Explore the available subscription, review its features and terms, then choose your plan in the app. Start with your goals and the information you have. Add supported connections or professional care when relevant to you.

Is ₹625 a monthly subscription?+

No. The annual price is ₹7,500, billed annually. ₹625 is its monthly equivalent, not a separate monthly payment option. Check the final billing, renewal and cancellation terms in the app before subscribing.
