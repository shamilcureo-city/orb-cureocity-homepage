Know what an insight is based on.

Sources, dates and missing information matter. A pattern can suggest a question; it does not prove the cause.

Share deliberately.

Choose relevant records or summaries for the people involved in your care. Sharing controls should make access understandable.

Know where AI stops.

AI helps organise information, explain context and support planning. Clinical diagnosis and treatment decisions belong with qualified professionals.
