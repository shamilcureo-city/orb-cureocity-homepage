A few things
before you begin.

Start with the information you have. Add the support you need.

Do I need a wearable?+

No. You can begin with tools that use your records and your own inputs. Device-based trends and scores require the relevant supported data; missing data should not appear as a zero or a complete picture.

Can I use my existing reports?+

Health Vault is designed to organise existing reports, keep the originals and let you review extracted information. Supported file formats, upload limits and release availability still need confirmation for this preview.

Does every plan include professionals?+

No. Professional services depend on the selected package and location. Check the included roles, consultations, service hours and follow-up arrangements before purchasing. Buying a service does not automatically schedule every appointment.

Can my family see everything?+

No. The Family Circle concept requires permission and is designed around selected summaries, with access you can revoke. It does not give unrestricted access to raw records or sensor streams. The demonstration above is illustrative; release availability is still to be confirmed.

Does Orb replace my doctor?+

No. Orb helps organise information and support daily follow-through. Clinical assessment, diagnosis and treatment decisions remain with appropriately qualified professionals.
