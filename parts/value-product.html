Your records keep their source.

The intended Health Vault flow keeps the original report and its date. Extracted details await your review before being treated as verified.

Available data adds context.

Supported wearable information and everyday logs can help you explore patterns. Missing information stays missing; a missing meal log does not mean a skipped meal.

You choose what to share.

The specified sharing flow lets you select records for a chosen consultant, limit read-only access and revoke it. AI explanations do not replace clinical decisions.
