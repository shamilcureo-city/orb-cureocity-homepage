- Source: supplied Orb Product and Website Brief, replacing prior wearable-first narrative.

- Care mode keyboard navigation, mode-specific copy and prerequisites.
- Family: no preselected permission, invitation acceptance followed by selection; revocation removes sample summary.
- Mobile navigation, download modal and Escape/focus return, FAQ disclosure; null destinations remain visibly unconfigured.
- Motion code uses reduced-motion and hidden-tab handling; transitions are finite.
- No actual health upload, sharing, AI processing, appointment booking, subscription or store download was performed.

Live store/policy/support destinations and approved product feature availability remain external launch inputs.
