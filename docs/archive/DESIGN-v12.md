# Orb complete product experience

The last version narrowed Orb to a meal/workout routine and hid the records, insights and care context that explain its breadth. The new site must make the whole app visible, let visitors recognise their own needs, and show practical value before the annual offer. A longer page is justified by distinct capabilities, not repeated sales copy.

Palette: Midnight #0b1427, Ink #172335, Cloud #f6f8fb, Ice #e5eef8, Coral #df526d, White #ffffff. Manrope headings with Inter body; retained brand assets and original navy-to-white gradient. Heading scale 40/32/24, readable 16px body, product labels 12px. Root content width1120; phones250-350px, not enlarged section-filling devices.

Composition: a centered opening and a layered original-product stage; a visible capability navigation and selectable visitor needs; food and movement product spreads; records and contextual insight scenes; cycle/journal detail; one connected-week example; professional care, assessments, family and data-source/control stories; detailed annual offer and FAQs.

Avoid copying Bevel's brand, claims, reviews or media. Its useful lesson is product visibility and depth. Orb's distinction is demonstrated continuity between personal context, daily activity and professional review. Do not claim exclusivity. Existing verified branding takes precedence over generic marketing styles. Keep core explanations visible, secondary technical details expandable, finite motion tied to meaningful transitions. Respect reduced-motion and keyboard use.

Price is confirmed at ₹7,500/year; ₹625 is a monthly equivalent billed annually. Care remains separately arranged. Final entitlements and release status are unconfirmed; retain owner-private audience and concise concept qualification. No fabricated ratings, testimonials, numerical health outcomes, compatibility claims or urgent scarcity.
